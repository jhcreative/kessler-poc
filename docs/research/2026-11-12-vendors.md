# Vendors in Dana's stack: first read (Cesar, 2026-11-12)

Public pages only, read 2026-11-12. Nothing here is confirmed by the client.

## FacilIT (Facil-IT, TMA Systems)

Source: https://info.getfacilit.com/commercial-service-provider-software

- Built for **commercial service providers, not facility owners**: multi-trade maintenance firms, HVAC, electrical,
  plumbing contractors serving national accounts; retail and restaurant maintenance specialists.
- "Take every job from work order to invoice in one flow": work orders, dispatch (techs and subcontractors),
  invoicing ("integrated accounting available in select editions"), contracts, SLAs, audit trails, subcontractor scoring.
- Connects to **client systems: ServiceChannel, Corrigo, Fexa**, "and other supported client systems". Mobile apps
  for techs, subs and clients.
- Not stated on the page: an API, pricing, estimates or not-to-exceed limits, photos, check-in/out.

**Read:** FacilIT looks like a full provider-side work-order-to-invoice platform with connectors into the chains'
facilities systems. That fits "some clients use it, OF pays to connect", and raises the question of why it isn't the
source of truth for everything.

## FieldDesk (fielddesk.io)

Source: https://fielddesk.io/ (page renders in JavaScript; read from its meta tags)

- Describes itself as an **"external operations desk"** that optimizes dispatch, scheduling and capacity management for
  field service companies, with **"expert-led process improvement."**
- That reads as an outsourced operations service (people and process), possibly with software, not plainly a SaaS
  system of record. Unconfirmed: needs the full page in a browser, or Dana's answer.
- Not the same product as the FieldDesk module of FieldOPS (a tech-side estimates and parts add-on), which search
  results also return.

## Samsara

Source: https://developers.samsara.com/docs/rest-api-overview

- Fleet telematics with a public REST API (bearer token): vehicle locations (current, history, and a cursor-based
  feed), drivers (create, read, update). Samsara points new work to its Vehicle Stats API.
- Likely enough for "where is each truck now" in a dispatch view. Parts inventory is not Samsara's job.
