A FIVE-MINUTE RUN-THROUGH

The app opens on 45 synthetic jobs at every stage, and nothing moves until you act. Open two roles side by side, each in its own private window, and follow one request through.

  1. 1 COORDINATORClick New request. Claude drafts it in seconds, labelled as a draft. Check it and accept it.
  2. 2 DISPATCHThe job lands in Ready on its own. Open it, read why the tech is recommended, and assign.
  3. 3 TECHOn a phone, the job is on the schedule. Work it, take the sign-off and close it.
  4. 4 CONTROLLERThe invoice arrives drafted and checked against the contract. Approve it for QuickBooks.
  5. 5 OVERVIEWEvery count and wait time moved as you went.
