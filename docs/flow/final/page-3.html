Single Source of Truth for Everyone

Proof of Concept•Kessler Facility Services•November 16, 2026

Assuming we've understood the current system correctly, we believe KFS can improve operations significantly and be much better prepared for efficient expansion by moving towards a 'single source of truth' system model.

While FieldDesk appears to maintain the closest thing to unified truth in the current system, and while FacilIT offers many of the features we'd like to see in unified truth, both are paid services and building on top of them locks in expense, contract commitments, etc. before thorough analysis is done.

Given unknowns, this POC has been conceived to demonstrate building a complete solution 'in-house', which we propose will be faster to market, easier to use, more reliable and more flexible in coming expansion than building on a third-party solution.

1WHY ONE RECORD

Detail is lost between people.

Requests arrive accurate enough. The detail goes missing in the handling: a notes field, a photo on a personal phone, a spreadsheet. One record holds the job's state, its contract and its history, where every role can find them.

2GUARDRAILS FIRST

The contract checks come before the work.

Each client's limits, like a price cap that needs prior approval, sit on the record before a tech is dispatched. Work held for the client's sign-off is work that never turns into a disputed invoice.

3SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH

Every change reaches every view right away.

Each role updates the record, and the record tells everyone else within a second. Software drafts and recommends; people review and decide. Nobody calls dispatch to learn where a job stands.
