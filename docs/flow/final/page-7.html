The Application Concept

Proof of Concept•OneFamilyServices•November 16, 2026

One shared job record, and a view for each role that works from it. The proof of concept runs the whole flow, from a new request to an invoice in QuickBooks, on synthetic data. Page 8 shows where each view sits.

1FIVE VIEWS

Each role sees its own work.

Coordinator, dispatch, tech, controller and an executive overview. The tech's phone shows only that tech's jobs; the overview shows them all.

2DRAFTS, THEN PEOPLE

Software drafts; people decide.

Claude drafts each request from its source, labelled as a draft. When it can't, the rules draft it and say why. A coordinator checks every draft before it moves.

3RULES THAT HOLD

Contracts stop disputes early.

Over a client's cap, dispatch waits until the client's approval is on record. An invoice that fails its check can't be approved.

4LIVE

Every change, in every view.

One view's change shows in every other open view within a second, with no reload. A view that drops its connection catches up on what it missed.

5THE CLOCK

Every step, timed.

The overview times each step from the record's own history. That's the Cost of Delay, measured, and the test of every efficiency on page 6.

6BUILT SMALL

Owned, tested, typed.

About 4,600 hand-written lines, tested at every layer and audited by a fresh reviewer. Types generated from the backend keep the screens in step.
