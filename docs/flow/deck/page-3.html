The certain win: approval in real time

When a job in the field needs client approval, over a cap or any rule that fires, the job updates at once. Dispatch re-plans the allocation, and the coordinator starts the approval right away instead of chasing it after the work. Today the chase comes after, and the dispute follows.

People decide

  • A person can review and override anything in the field.
  • Every confirm, deny and override is recorded.
  • A person reviews what the system learned before it changes future predictions.

Rails nobody overrides

  • Price caps.
  • Every other stipulation in the client's contract.

Still open

  • Which contract terms the record must carry first.
  • What makes two jobs "like" for the predicted range.
